Website Goals and Objectives 

Goals for website: 

    Make website effortlessly obtainable when searching for it on the internet by utilizing SEO  

    Make it easy for users to understand content on the website 

    Produce a user-friendly website that will urge new patients to make appointment bookings  

    Credibility (Hosting and domain) 

Performance KPIs to measure success: 

    Tracking website: Monitoring how many patients book appointments. 

    Monitor appointment no shows: Help business figure out where they lack and how they can do better.  

    Identify dentistry's business costs:  which will help understand where money goes and help business allocate funds accordingly  

Current Website Analysis 

    Iron Crown Dentistry doesn’t have an existing website. 

Proposed website features and functionality 

The website features and functionality that the Iron Crown will have are:   

    A landing page (Home page) 

    About us (Details about business including mission and vision statements) 

    Testimonials (Reviews from clients) 

    Services Page (The services the practice is going to offer) 

    Enquire Page  

    Interactive tool (WhatsApp feature for easier communication)

    Appointment booking page 

 

 Design and user experience   

    Iron Crown Dentistry will have cool green soft tones. The website will have polished and concise fonts like raleway and mulish fonts that are both elegant and modern.  

    The goal is to have a website that is calm, inviting, friendly, to help users not deal with a confusing website when they already have stress about their teeth.
